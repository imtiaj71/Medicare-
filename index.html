Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order medicine?

Search for a medicine or upload a prescription. The live website should then confirm availability, delivery details, and checkout.

Can I pay by bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or card?

This design displays those payment options. Real payments require a merchant account and integration with an approved payment gateway.

Can I order prescription-only medicine?

Prescription medicines must be reviewed and dispensed according to applicable Bangladesh laws and pharmacy requirements. Do not upload or share prescriptions on this demo page.
